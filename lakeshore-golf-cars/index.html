Your ads

You’re paying to send people to your site, then losing them there.

When I loaded lakeshoregolfcars.com on November 12, scrolled to the bottom and waited, no request went to Facebook at all and no Google tag loaded. Your site has $12,999 Evolution Mavericks in stock and a Get A Quote form, which are exactly the pages that show who’s serious.

Step 1A Northshore shopper taps your “Evolution is back” ad
Step 2They land on your site, look at a Maverick, maybe start a quote
Step 3They leave. With no Meta pixel, Facebook can’t bring them back
The fix: connect the Meta pixel through Shopify’s Facebook & Instagram app (about 15 minutes), build a retargeting audience of everyone who viewed a cart or the financing page, and count Get A Quote submissions as leads so Facebook learns who actually buys.
