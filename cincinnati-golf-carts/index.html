Your ads

You’re paying for the attention, then losing the shopper.

When I loaded cincinnatigolfcarts.com on November 12, scrolled to the bottom and waited, no Meta pixel fired and no Facebook request went out at all. Your inventory pages are exactly where serious buyers show themselves, and right now Facebook can’t see who visited them.

Step 1A Cincinnati shopper sees your $5,750 E-Z-GO ad
Step 2They look you up and browse your 16 carts in stock
Step 3They leave. With no Meta pixel, Facebook can’t bring them back
The fix: add the Meta pixel to your site (about 15 minutes), build a retargeting audience of everyone who viewed a cart, financing or contact page, and let Facebook learn who actually buys so it can find more people like them.

Meta Ad Library and a live check of cincinnatigolfcarts.com, November 12, 2026.

2

Your website

A shopper at 10 p.m. has no way to raise a hand.

Your Contact page says calling or texting is the fastest way to reach you, and that works for people ready to talk. I found no form on the homepage or the Contact page, so anyone browsing late, at work, or not ready for a call has nowhere to leave a name and number.

The fix: add one short “Ask about this cart” form (name, phone, which cart) to each listing and the Contact page, sent straight to your phone. Pair it with the pixel so form fills count as leads in your ads.
