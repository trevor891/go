A competitor with half your reviews is paying to sit on top of you

When a visitor searches "golf cart rental port aransas," Kacie's Beach Carts shows up twice: once as a paid ad at the top and once below it. Sage, with nearly twice as many reviews, is the last of the four businesses Google shows.

There's also no tracking installed on sagebeachcarts.com, so if Sage ran an ad like Kacie's, there would be no way to tell which bookings it brought in.

The fix: install tracking first, then put your review count to work where people choose a cart company: your Google profile, your booking page, and a small test ad.
2

Listings

Five websites still send people to your old address

Yelp, BBB, Birdeye, CitySquares and MerchantCircle still list Sage at the Highway 361 address, not the in-town office. The Yelp page has never been claimed and shows 3.7 stars from 9 reviews, while Google shows 4.8 from more than a thousand.

What 5 directories show5009 Highway 361
Where you actually are3417 S 11th St, Port Aransas

Google ranks local businesses partly on whether their address matches across the web, and a tourist who trusts the wrong listing drives to the wrong place.

The fix: claim and correct all five listings, then add an automatic review request after every rental so the best reviews keep coming in.

Directory listings checked October 25, 2026.

3

Cart sales

December is a selling month you're not using yet

Every golf cart plate in Port Aransas expires on December 31 and has to be renewed by January 1. Every private cart owner on the island is thinking about their cart this month. Your site already lists retired 4-seaters at $5,000.

10 retired carts × $5,000 = $50,000 in sales

Simple arithmetic on your listed price, not a forecast.

The fix: an end-of-season fleet sale plus a "Ready for 2027" service special, promoted to locals, homeowners and past renters who agreed to hear from you.
