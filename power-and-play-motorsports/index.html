Local search

A shop with 3 reviews, down the same street, shows up instead of you

3 reviews · 541 S Flagler Ave

Google search on November 2, 2026. Results vary by searcher and location.

For "golf carts for sale pompano beach fl," Google shows BMK (964 reviews), Sunshine (241), South Florida Golf Car (3 reviews, on S Flagler) and a paid spot for Phoenix Golf Car. Power & Play, with 225 reviews a few blocks up Flagler, isn't there.

When a dealer with real reviews is missing, the usual cause is how Google categorizes the business. If your profile reads as a scooter or motorsports shop, Google doesn't consider you for golf cart searches.

The fix: add "Golf cart dealer" as a category on your Google profile, add your golf carts as products with photos and prices, and post a golf cart photo weekly. Ask golf cart buyers to mention the cart in their review.
2

Your ads

Your website can't see who your ads send it

Your Facebook ad has been running since July 13 with a Send Message button. Your website at powerandplaymotorsports.com has a financing page, inventory and a Google tag, but no Meta pixel. Your Facebook page also doesn't link to the website.

So everyone who sees the ad, gets curious and visits the site is invisible to Facebook. You can't show them the ad again, and Facebook can't learn which people actually buy.

The fix: install the Meta pixel on the GoDaddy site (about 15 minutes), add the website link to your Facebook page, and set up a retargeting audience so the people who looked at a golf cart or the financing page see you again that week.
